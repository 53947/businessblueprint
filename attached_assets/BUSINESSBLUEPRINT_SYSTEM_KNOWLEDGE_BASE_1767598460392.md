## 📊 THE DIGITAL IQ ASSESSMENT SYSTEM

### Purpose
Measure a business's online presence effectiveness across 9 critical areas and generate a Digital IQ Score (0-140 points) that becomes the foundation for prescriptions.

### The 9 Core Areas We Measure

**1. Website & SEO Fundamentals (20 points max)**
- Purpose: Assess digital home base effectiveness
- Measured by: Automated scanner + user questions
- Key metrics: SSL, mobile-friendly, load time, SEO score, content completeness

**2. Business Listings & Citations (18 points max)**
- Purpose: Ensure business is findable across directories
- Measured by: Automated scanner (Google, Yelp, Yellow Pages, BBB, Facebook)
- Key metrics: Active listings, claimed status, NAP consistency

**3. Google Business Profile Optimization (16 points max)**
- Purpose: Maximize local search visibility
- Measured by: Google Places API + user questions
- Key metrics: Profile completeness, post recency, photo recency, verification

**4. Reputation Management (16 points max)**
- Purpose: Build trust through review engagement
- Measured by: Review platforms + user questions
- Key metrics: Response recency, response rate, new review recency

**5. Review Quality & Quantity (15 points max)**
- Purpose: Social proof that drives decisions
- Measured by: Google & Yelp APIs
- Key metrics: Total reviews, average rating, response rate

**6. Customer Response & Timing (15 points max)**
- Purpose: Capture leads before competitors
- Measured by: User questions only
- Key metrics: Response speed, unified inbox, consistency

**7. Email & SMS Marketing (15 points max)**
- Purpose: Monetize existing relationships
- Measured by: User questions only
- Key metrics: List size, campaign recency, SMS capability

**8. Social Media Content (13 points max)**
- Purpose: Stay top-of-mind with audience
- Measured by: Presence scanner + user questions
- Key metrics: Post recency, post frequency, active profiles

**9. CRM: Theory, Setup & Execution (12 points max)**
- Purpose: Systematize and scale customer relationships
- Measured by: User questions only
- Key metrics: CRM usage, follow-up recency, automation

**Total: 140 points = Digital IQ Score**

## 🎯 DIGITAL IQ SCORING CALCULATION

### Scoring Formula (0-140 points)

Each of the 9 areas is scored 0-100, then weighted to produce final points:

```
Website & SEO Score: (0-100) × 0.20 = 0-20 points
Listings Score: (0-100) × 0.18 = 0-18 points
GBP Score: (0-100) × 0.16 = 0-16 points
Reputation Mgmt Score: (0-100) × 0.16 = 0-16 points
Reviews Score: (0-100) × 0.15 = 0-15 points
Response Timing Score: (0-100) × 0.15 = 0-15 points
Email/SMS Score: (0-100) × 0.15 = 0-15 points
Social Media Score: (0-100) × 0.13 = 0-13 points
CRM Score: (0-100) × 0.12 = 0-12 points

DIGITAL IQ SCORE = Sum of all weighted scores (0-140)
```

### Grade Ranges

- **126-140 (A+):** Excellent digital presence
- **112-125 (A):** Strong digital presence
- **98-111 (B+):** Good digital presence
- **84-97 (B):** Above average
- **70-83 (C+):** Average
- **56-69 (C):** Below average
- **42-55 (D+):** Needs significant improvement
- **28-41 (D):** Poor digital presence
- **0-27 (F):** Critical issues

## 📝 THE FOUR BUSINESS SCENARIOS

### How We Classify Businesses

Every business falls into one of four scenarios based on TWO factors:
1. **Stage:** New vs. Established
2. **Priority:** Need cash fast vs. Need proper setup

### Scenario 1: New Business - Need Cash Fast
**Characteristics:**
- Just starting (0-6 months)
- Low/no revenue
- Need money in register quickly
- Willing to work intensively

**Prescription Focus (6-week timeline):**
1. Google Business Profile Optimization (Week 1)
2. Reputation Management - Get 5-10 reviews FAST (Weeks 1-2)
3. Customer Response & Timing (Week 1)
4. Live Chat (Week 2)
5. Email/SMS to Existing Network (Week 1)

**NOT priorities:**
- Social media content (takes months)
- SEO (takes 3-6 months)
- CRM setup (slows them down)
- Perfect website (good enough works)

**AI Instruction:** Prioritize immediate revenue drivers. Every recommendation must show path to cash within 6 weeks.

---

### Scenario 2: New Business - Need Setup
**Characteristics:**
- Just starting (0-6 months)
- Have runway (3-6 months cash)
- Want to build foundation correctly
- Patient, strategic mindset

**Prescription Focus (3-6 month timeline):**
1. Business Listings & Citations (Complete and accurate everywhere)
2. Website & SEO Fundamentals (Built right with technical SEO)
3. Google Business Profile (Fully optimized, not just claimed)
4. Social Media Presence (All platforms secured, branded consistently)
5. CRM Setup (Start organized from customer #1)
6. Email/SMS Systems (Automation ready)
7. Reputation Management (Review collection systems)
8. Response Systems (Inbox, live chat infrastructure)
9. Social Media Content Strategy (Calendar, templates, processes)

**AI Instruction:** Build proper foundation. Emphasize systems, automation, and scalability. Think long-term.

---

### Scenario 3: Established Business - Need Cash Fast
**Characteristics:**
- Operating 1+ years
- Revenue stagnant or declining
- Losing money/leads
- Urgent turnaround needed

**Prescription Focus (30-45 day timeline):**
1. Reputation Management (Fix what's hurting you NOW)
2. Customer Response & Timing (Stop losing leads)
3. Email & SMS to Existing Customers (Fastest money)
4. Live Chat (Capture existing traffic)
5. Google Business Profile (Quick wins)

**NOT priorities:**
- Social media content
- Website redesign
- CRM migration
- New citations

**AI Instruction:** Plug the leaks FIRST. Focus on monetizing existing assets (customer list, website traffic, brand awareness). Quick wins that generate cash.

---

### Scenario 4: Established Business - Need Setup/Optimization
**Characteristics:**
- Operating 1+ years
- Stable revenue
- Want to systematize and scale
- Growth-focused, not survival mode

**Prescription Focus (3-6 month timeline):**
1. CRM Implementation (Organize existing chaos)
2. Email & SMS Automation (Scale communication)
3. Social Media Content Systems (Consistent presence)
4. Business Listings Audit (Fix inconsistencies)
5. Website & SEO Optimization (Improve what exists)
6. Reputation Management Systems (Automate review collection)
7. Response Systems Integration (Unified inbox across channels)
8. Live Chat Optimization (Better scripts, routing)
9. Google Business Profile Enhancement (Posts, updates, Q&A)

**AI Instruction:** Systematize what's working. Focus on automation, efficiency, and scalability. Turn chaos into process.

## 💊 PRESCRIPTION GENERATION SYSTEM

### What Is A Prescription?

A prescription is a customized, phased implementation roadmap that tells a business:
1. What to fix (based on their Digital IQ assessment)
2. In what order (based on their scenario)
3. How to do it (specific, actionable steps)
4. Why it matters (revenue/growth impact)

### Perfect Prescription Criteria

**Concise:**
- Clear, actionable recommendations
- No walls of text or fluff
- Specific steps, not vague advice

**Timely:**
- Delivered within 2-3 minutes of assessment completion
- Appropriate to their current situation
- Aligned with their timeline (6 weeks vs. 6 months)

**Accurate:**
- Based on real data (their assessment + industry benchmarks)
- Scenario-appropriate (new/established, cash/setup)
- Prioritized by impact

### Three-Phase Delivery Framework

Every prescription is broken into 3 phases:

**Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1)**
- Goal: Establish presence, get found locally
- Focus: Critical gaps that prevent discovery
- Examples: Claim GBP, fix citations, get first reviews

**Phase 2: Engagement (Months 2-3)**
- Goal: Build trust, capture leads
- Focus: Convert traffic to customers
- Examples: Live chat, review responses, response time

**Phase 3: Growth (Months 4-6)**
- Goal: Nurture, convert, scale
- Focus: Systematize and automate
- Examples: Email campaigns, CRM, automation

**CRITICAL:** Phase priorities change based on scenario:
- **New + Cash:** Phase 1 = Revenue drivers (reviews, response, GBP)
- **New + Setup:** Phase 1 = Foundation (citations, website, GBP)
- **Established + Cash:** Phase 1 = Plug leaks (reputation, response, email)
- **Established + Setup:** Phase 1 = Systematize (CRM, automation, audit)

### AI Provider Strategy

**Multi-AI Provider System:**
- **DeepSeek:** Assessment analysis + prescription generation (cost-effective, 90% savings)
- **Claude:** Coach Blue coaching interactions (premium conversational quality)
- **OpenAI:** Fallback for all features (balanced performance)

**Why This Matters:**
- DeepSeek excels at analytical tasks with structured output
- Claude excels at empathetic, motivational coaching
- Cost optimization: ~$0.27/M tokens (DeepSeek) vs. $3-15/M tokens (Claude)

**Admin Control:**
- Settings managed via `/admin` AI Settings panel
- Can switch providers without code changes
- Automatic fallback if primary provider fails

## 🔄 THE COMPLETE WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Assessment Submission

**User Journey:**
1. User lands on `/assessment` page
2. Completes business information form (basic data + 27 questions)
3. Clicks "Submit Assessment"
4. Sees confirmation screen with Assessment ID

**System Actions:**
1. Validates all form data
2. Creates assessment record in database
3. Triggers presence scanner (website, social, directories, reviews)
4. Calls Google Places API for business data
5. Calls Yelp API for business data
6. Generates 15-minute temporary access token

**Email Sent:**
- **Assessment Confirmation Email** (immediate)
  - Subject: "✓ Assessment Received - BusinessBlueprint.io"
  - Content: Professional confirmation with timeline
  - Branded: Light blue background, Archivo fonts, blueprint grid
  - CTA: "Check Your Email" (for prescription when ready)

---

### Step 2: AI Analysis & Prescription Generation

**System Actions (automated, 2-3 minutes):**
1. Combines all data sources (form answers + scanner results + APIs)
2. Calculates Digital IQ Score (0-140) across 9 areas
3. Determines business scenario (New/Established + Cash/Setup)
4. Sends to AI provider (DeepSeek by default)

**AI Analysis Process:**
```
INPUT:
- Digital IQ Score: 67/140 (Grade: C)
- Scenario: Established + Need Cash
- Weaknesses: Low reviews (8/35), slow response (6/15), no email list (2/15)
- Strengths: Good website (18/20), active GBP (14/16)

AI INSTRUCTIONS:
- Generate 3-phase prescription
- Focus Phase 1 on revenue drivers (reviews, response, email)
- Provide specific, actionable steps
- Explain WHY each recommendation matters
- Estimate effort and impact for each

OUTPUT:
- Prescription summary
- 6-12 prioritized recommendations
- Phase 1, 2, 3 breakdown
- Quick wins identified
- Estimated timeline
```

**Data Storage:**
1. Saves to `prescriptions` table:
   - prescriptionId
   - assessmentId (linked)
   - clientId (null until account created)
   - title: "Digital Growth Prescription for [Business Name]"
   - summary: Executive overview
   - status: 'delivered'
   - deliveredAt: timestamp

2. Saves to `recommendations` table (each recommendation):
   - recommendationId
   - assessmentId (linked)
   - category (which of 9 areas)
   - title
   - description
   - priority (high/medium/low)
   - estimatedImpact
   - estimatedEffort
   - phase (1, 2, or 3)

---
