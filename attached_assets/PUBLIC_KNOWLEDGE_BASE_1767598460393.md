## 📊 Your Digital IQ Score

### What Is It?

Your Digital IQ Score (0-140 points) measures how effectively your business shows up online and converts visitors into customers.

### The 9 Areas We Measure

**1. Website & SEO Fundamentals (20 points)**
- Is your website fast, secure, and mobile-friendly?
- Can people find you on Google?

**2. Business Listings & Citations (18 points)**
- Are you listed on Google, Yelp, and other directories?
- Is your information consistent everywhere?

**3. Google Business Profile (16 points)**
- Is your profile complete and optimized?
- Do you post updates and photos regularly?

**4. Reputation Management (16 points)**
- Do you respond to reviews?
- Are you actively managing your online reputation?

**5. Review Quality & Quantity (15 points)**
- How many reviews do you have?
- What's your average rating?

**6. Customer Response & Timing (15 points)**
- How quickly do you respond to inquiries?
- Do you have systems to capture every lead?

**7. Email & SMS Marketing (15 points)**
- Do you have a customer email list?
- Do you send regular campaigns?

**8. Social Media Content (13 points)**
- Are you active on social media?
- Do you post regularly?

**9. CRM & Organization (12 points)**
- Do you track customer relationships?
- Are you organized and systematic?

**126-140 (A+):** Excellent - You're a digital leader  
**112-125 (A):** Strong - Minor improvements needed
